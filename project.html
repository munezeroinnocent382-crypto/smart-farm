Agriculture Technology/ Smart Farm

Smart Irrigation

Soil-moisture sensors detect when crops need water. Irrigation automatically supplies the required amount of water. Helps reduce water waste.

AI Crop Monitoring

Use cameras or other sensors to monitor crops. AI can help identify crop diseases, pests, and unhealthy plants.

Agricultural Mechanization

Provide modern machinery and mechanization services. Examples: tractors, planters, harvesters, and other farm equipment.

Smart Soil Monitoring

Sensors can monitor soil moisture, temperature, and other conditions. Farmers can use the information to make better decisions.

Farmer Training

Teach farmers how to use AI and modern agricultural technologies. Train young people in smart farming and agricultural mechanization

💡 Simple Example

Traditional method:

Farmer checks the field manually → decides when to irrigate → discovers disease after seeing damaged plants.

Smart Farm Rwanda method:

Sensors → Data → AI/System → Farmer → Action

For example:

🌱 Soil sensor detects that the soil is dry.

📱 Farmer receives information.

💧 Irrigation system supplies water.

📷 Camera monitors the tomato plants.

🤖 AI detects possible disease symptoms.

👨‍🌾 Farmer receives an alert and takes action early.
